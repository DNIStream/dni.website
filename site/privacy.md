This privacy policy sets out how **DNI Stream** uses and protects any information that you provide
when you use this website.

**DNI Stream** is committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected. Should we ask you to provide
certain information by which you can be identified when using this website, you can be assured that
it will only be used in accordance with this privacy statement.

We may change this policy from time to time by updating this page. This policy is effective from
**5 October 2018** and was last modified on **11 October 2026**.

## What we collect

When you send us a message from the [contact page](/contact/), we collect:

- your name
- your email address
- anything else you write in your message

## What we do with it

We use it only to reply to your message. We do not use it for marketing, and we will not sell,
distribute or lease it to third parties unless we have your permission or are required by law to do
so.

## Services this website uses

- **Web3Forms** delivers contact form messages to our inbox. What you send through the form passes
  through Web3Forms; see the [Web3Forms privacy policy](https://web3forms.com/privacy).
- **hCaptcha**, provided through Web3Forms, checks that a message was sent by a person. It runs only
  on the contact page; see the [hCaptcha privacy policy](https://www.hcaptcha.com/privacy).
- **Fireside**, our podcast host, serves the episode audio and artwork. When you play an episode or
  view its artwork, your browser fetches it from Fireside; see the
  [Fireside privacy policy](https://fireside.fm/privacy).
- **Plausible Analytics** counts page visits so we can see which pages are useful. It sets no cookies,
  stores nothing on your device and collects no personal data; see the
  [Plausible data policy](https://plausible.io/data-policy).

## Cookies and storage

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. The hCaptcha check on the contact
page may set the cookies it needs to work; see its privacy policy above.

## Links to other websites

Our website contains links to other websites of interest. Once you have used these links to leave our
site, we do not have any control over that other website, so we are not responsible for the
protection and privacy of any information which you provide whilst visiting it. Look at the privacy
statement applicable to the website in question.

## Controlling your personal information

You may request details of personal information which we hold about you under the
[Data Protection Act 2018](https://www.gov.uk/data-protection). If you would like a copy of the
information held on you, or believe that any of it is incorrect or incomplete, please
[contact us](/contact/). We will promptly correct any information found to be incorrect.
